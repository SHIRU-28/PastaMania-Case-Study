# PastaMania World Pasta Day '25 Campaign Case Study

**Role:** Social Media Manager & Campaign Strategist  
**Campaign:** World Pasta Day 2025  
**Client:** PastaMania Sri Lanka

## Overview
A multi-format organic campaign delivering 24,500+ engagements on zero paid spend through viral reel strategy and community engagement initiatives.

## Key Results
- **24,500+** organic engagements
- **Zero** ad spend
- **National** branch distribution
- Successful community breakfast event with Urban Council

## Campaign Highlights
- Guerrilla viral reel strategy with local hooks
- 4 exclusive combo pack launches
- Community engagement with street cleaners
- Strategic brand positioning without over-promotion
